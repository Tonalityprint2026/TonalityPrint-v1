	Annotations:
* Primary Labels (5): Five core functional Tonal Intent States (Trust, Attention, Reciprocity, Empathy Resonance, Cognitive Energy)
* Secondary Labels (24): Twenty-Four total optional sub-modifiers providing fine-grained descriptors
*  Baseline/Neutral (1): control sample; default prosody for comparative analysis), 
Cross-Intent Optional Sub-Modifier (1):
* Ambivalence (if applicable, across all five Tonal Intent States)
Intent-Specific Optional Sub-Modifiers (24):
1. Trust (5): Authoritative, Calm, Confident, Formal/Respectful, Reassuring
2. Attention (5):Certainty, Clarity, Curious, Focused, Urgent/Pressure
3. Reciprocity (5): Affirming, Collaborative, Engaged, Open, Reflective
4. Empathy Resonance (5): Casual, Compassion, Corrective (softened), Sympathetic, Warm
5. Cognitive Energy (4): Analytical, Dynamic, Enthusiastic, Skeptical

The first letter of the 5 Primary Tonal States is capitalized
E.g.,  Att , Cogen


All of the optional sub-modifiers letters are all lowercase 
E.g., warm, curi , ana


Ambivalence, the optional cross-modifier is annotated as all lowercase 
E.g., ambivalex 


The first letter of Baseline / Neutral is capitalized
E.g., Baseneutral
